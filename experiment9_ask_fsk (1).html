MANDATORY VALIDATION

Calculate the inner product of the two BFSK basis signals, φ1(t)=cos(2πf1t) and φ0(t)=cos(2πf0t), over one bit interval Tb, as a function of tone spacing Δf. Orthogonality (zero inner product) is what lets a correlator receiver tell the two tones apart with no cross-talk.

Inner product ⟨φ₁,φ₀⟩ vs. tone spacing Δf

The curve crosses zero at Δf = k·0.5·Rb for integer k. The smallest of these, Δf = 0.5·Rb, is the minimum tone spacing for coherent BFSK orthogonality (Sunde's FSK). Noncoherent (envelope) detection needs the stricter Δf = 1.0·Rb to avoid cross-talk between the two energy detectors.
